# 🌾 Kisaan Bharosa

> **AI-Powered Fake Seed & Fertilizer Detector for Pakistani Farmers**
> 
> *Built for AI Hackathon Pakistan 2026 by Muhammad Huzaifa*

## 🚨 The Problem

Fake seeds, counterfeit fertilizer, and adulterated pesticides are destroying crops and ruining livelihoods across Pakistan. Small farmers have **no way to verify** product authenticity before purchase.

## ✅ Our Solution

**Kisaan Bharosa** lets any farmer verify agricultural products using just their phone camera.

1. **📸 Snap** — Take a photo of any fertilizer or seed bag
2. **🤖 Analyze** — AI checks 6 critical authenticity markers in seconds
3. **📋 Result** — Get instant "Real / Fake / Uncertain" verdict with confidence score
4. **🚨 Report** — Flag counterfeit products to protect the community
